CampusFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

In short: CampusFlow is designed to store your academic, placement and internship records locally on your device. The app does not require a user account. Internship discovery uses an external job data service to retrieve opportunities and requires an internet connection. Please refer to the sections below for further information about local storage, external services and notifications.

Information stored on your device

CampusFlow stores the following in the app's private storage on your iPhone:

  • Assignments, their tasks, due dates, priorities and notes
  • Subjects you create
  • Placements, their dates and descriptions, and their requirements
  • Internships you save, and your application status for each
  • PDF documents you attach to assignments

CampusFlow uses local storage for supported academic records, placement information, saved internships and PDF documents. The app does not require users to connect their university accounts.

Importing PDFs from other apps

If you share a PDF to CampusFlow from another app, CampusFlow's share extension uses a small list of your assignment titles, kept in storage shared only between CampusFlow and its extension on your device, so you can choose which assignment the PDF belongs to. The PDF is copied into CampusFlow's storage on your device.

Internship search (Discovery)

Internship search (Discovery)

CampusFlow uses a third-party job data service, CleanJobData, to retrieve internship opportunities. Using the Discovery feature requires an internet connection and involves sending search requests to the external service.

These requests may include search terms, filters and other technical information needed to retrieve results. The external service may also receive connection information, such as your IP address.

CampusFlow stores academic records, placement information and supporting documents locally. These records are not intended to be included in internship search requests.

When you open a company website from a job listing, you leave CampusFlow and access an external website that operates under its own privacy practices.

Notifications

CampusFlow asks for permission to send notifications so it can remind you about requirement due dates. Reminders are created and delivered on your device. You can turn notifications off at any time in Settings > Notifications > CampusFlow.

What CampusFlow does not do

  • It does not require an account or ask for your name or email address.
  • It does not use advertising, analytics or tracking, and does not sell or share your data.
  • It does not access your camera, microphone, photos, location or contacts.
  • It does not connect to your university, and it does not confirm that any placement requirement has been approved. Always confirm requirements through your university's official process.

Your choices

You can manage or delete supported records within CampusFlow.Uninstalling the app removes its local app data from your device, although copies retained in device backups may remain subject to your device's backup settings.

Changes to this policy

If CampusFlow changes how it handles information, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Please open an issue on GitHub.

CampusFlow is a student project and is not affiliated with any university or career website.
