CampusFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

In short: CampusFlow has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your planner information stays on your device. The only information that leaves your device is the search you type into the Discovery screen.

Information stored on your device

CampusFlow stores the following in the app's private storage on your iPhone:

  • Assignments, their tasks, due dates, priorities and notes
  • Subjects you create
  • Placements, their dates and descriptions, and their requirements
  • Internships you save, and your application status for each
  • PDF documents you attach to assignments

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else, and CampusFlow has no servers of its own. If you delete CampusFlow, this information is deleted with it.

Importing PDFs from other apps

If you share a PDF to CampusFlow from another app, CampusFlow's share extension uses a small list of your assignment titles, kept in storage shared only between CampusFlow and its extension on your device, so you can choose which assignment the PDF belongs to. The PDF is copied into CampusFlow's storage on your device.

Internship search (Discovery)

When you search or browse on the Discovery screen, CampusFlow sends a request to a third-party job data service, CleanJobData, to get internship listings. The request contains your search words, whether you chose remote opportunities only, a country filter, and the page of results requested. Like any website or service you connect to, the provider can also see your device's IP address.

CampusFlow does not send your name, assignments, placements, documents or any other information from your planner. Please see CleanJobData's own privacy policy for how it handles requests.

The "Company Website" button opens the company's website in your browser. That website is run by the company and has its own privacy policy.

Notifications

CampusFlow asks for permission to send notifications so it can remind you about requirement due dates. Reminders are created and delivered on your device. You can turn notifications off at any time in Settings > Notifications > CampusFlow.

What CampusFlow does not do

  • It does not require an account or ask for your name or email address.
  • It does not use advertising, analytics or tracking, and does not sell or share your data.
  • It does not access your camera, microphone, photos, location or contacts.
  • It does not connect to your university, and it does not confirm that any placement requirement has been approved. Always confirm requirements through your university's official process.

Your choices

You can edit or delete any item in the app at any time. Deleting CampusFlow removes everything it stores on your device.

Changes to this policy

If CampusFlow changes how it handles information, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Please open an issue on GitHub.

CampusFlow is a student project and is not affiliated with any university or career website.
