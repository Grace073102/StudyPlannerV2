CampusFlow Support

Need help using CampusFlow? Start with these answers, or get in touch below.

Using the app

How do I manage my assignments?

Open the Assignments tab to add, edit and track your assignments and their tasks.

How do I add a subject?

In the Assignments tab, tap the books icon in the top corner to add, edit or delete subjects. You can then link a subject to an assignment or a placement.

How do I manage placement requirements?

Open the Career tab, choose Placements, tap your placement and then select Manage Requirements.

How do I edit or delete a placement?

Swipe a placement to the left to edit or delete it, or open it and tap the pencil to edit. Deleting a placement also deletes its requirements.

How do I find and save internships?

Open the Discovery tab to search for internships, then tap Save on one you like. Saved internships appear in the Career tab under Internships, where you can update your application status.

Why did a "Company Website" button open a different page than I expected?

Company websites come from a third-party service and may be out of date or missing for some listings. If a button is not shown, no website was available for that listing.

Reminders and data

How do reminders work?

CampusFlow can send reminders for requirement due dates. Allow notifications for CampusFlow in Settings > Notifications if you want them.

Does CampusFlow check my placement requirements with my university?

No. CampusFlow is a personal organiser. It does not connect to your university, and ticking a requirement does not mean your university has approved it. Always confirm requirements and submit documents through your university's official process.

Where is my data stored?

Your assignments, placements and requirements are stored on your device. See the Privacy page for details.

Contact support

Found a problem or have a question? Please open an issue on GitHub and include what you were doing and what you expected to happen.

CampusFlow is a student project and is not affiliated with any university or career website.
