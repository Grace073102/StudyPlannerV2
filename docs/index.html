Stay organised with CampusFlow

CampusFlow is an iOS student planner that brings academic tasks, placement preparation and internship opportunities together in one app.

Features

  • Manage assignments and deadlines
  • Track tasks and academic progress
  • Organise placements and requirements
  • Discover and save internship opportunities
  • Manage supporting PDF documents

Coming soon to the App Store. CampusFlow is a student project and is not affiliated with any university or career website. Internship listings are provided by a third-party service and may change or be removed.
