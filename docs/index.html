University life is more than attending classes and completing assignments. Managing overlapping deadlines, preparing for professional placements and searching for internships can quickly become overwhelming.

CampusFlow brings academic planning, placement management and career opportunity discovery together in one student-focused iOS productivity app.

One App, Multiple Responsibilities

CampusFlow connects academic planning, placement and internship preparation, and career opportunities in one centralised experience.

Designed for university students across different disciplines, CampusFlow supports academic and professional preparation independently of university-specific systems.

Designed for Student Productivity

CampusFlow supports offline access to locally stored academic, placement and internship records. Personal records and supporting PDF documents remain stored on device, while internship discovery requires an internet connection.

The app also supports iOS accessibility features, including VoiceOver, alongside Light and Dark Mode.
